- Nvidia's AI advantage, traditionally seen as stemming solely from its GPUs, is now expanding beyond core chip manufacturing.
- The company is strategically shifting to provide integrated hardware systems and orchestration solutions vital for efficient megascale AI data centers.
- This new approach, exemplified by architectures like Vera Rubin, aims to solidify Nvidia's leadership by optimizing the entire AI compute infrastructure.
