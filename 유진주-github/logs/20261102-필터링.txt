AI can make tech CEOs’ lives easier. What about everyone else?
Anthony Ha
12:57 PM PST · November 1, 2026
Dazzle AI CEO Marissa Mayer recently became the latest tech CEO to face criticism over seemingly out-of-touch comments extolling the usefulness of AI.
In Mayer’s case, that involved
asking an AI tool for help with a school-assigned description of her daughter
. For OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, that’s included
suggesting that parents create personalized podcasts for their kids’ drive to school
and also declaring that
he could not imagine parenting without ChatGPT
. Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg has even suggested that it will become increasingly acceptable to
meet your social and romantic needs through AI
.
This all led TechCrunch’s Amanda Silberling to wonder if AI executives are
simply too out-of-touch to build products for normal people
— a crucial question with
Anthropic set to go public
just as Wall Street begins
losing its appetite for heavy AI spending
.
On the latest episode of
the Equity podcast
, Kirsten Korosec, Sean O’Kane, and I chatted with Amanda about why industry leaders can sometimes seem so tone deaf. Kirsten wondered if this is more of a marketing problem or a product problem.
Amanda’s answer: “A little bit of both.” She also argued that the tech industry is increasingly pushing a future “where basic human interaction gets more and more optimized out of actually occurring,” adding, “And call me old school, but I think that humans being able to talk to each other is probably a net positive for society.”

our conversation
, edited for length and clarity.
