### 요약 (Original)
- Grok, backed by Elon Musk, released the first 7 minutes of its AI-generated "Odyssey," three months after Musk promised a full-length, "historically accurate" movie.
- This follows Musk's earlier criticism of Christopher Nolan's highly successful, practical filmmaking "Odyssey," which garnered immense box office and critical acclaim.
- The author found Grok's AI clip surprisingly impressive in parts but questioned its historical accuracy, implicitly contrasting it with Nolan's established cinematic achievement.

- An Apollo Global Management economist claims AI has delivered "zero financial payoff" to most companies outside the tech industry.
- Analyzing the S&P 493, he found no evidence of AI improving profit margins or revenue per worker.
- He argues AI lacks human judgment, accountability, and ethical understanding crucial for driving real business value beyond mere calculation.
