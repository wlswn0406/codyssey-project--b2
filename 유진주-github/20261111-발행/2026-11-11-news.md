- New York's RAISE Act, a significant AI safety law, mandates large frontier AI developers to register and adhere to safety and transparency requirements by January 1, 2027.
- Companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, with over $500 million in annual revenue, must publish safety frameworks and report critical incidents within 72 hours.

### 요약 (Original)
- Meta is expanding its AI agent Muse to Apple Watch and Android Wear OS, enabling users to interact via voice, text, or dictation directly on their smartwatches.
- This move, following a dedicated iPad app launch, highlights Meta's ambition to integrate Muse deeply into its consumer AI ecosystem.
- Muse offers diverse functionalities, from managing daily tasks to booking reservations, and recently added features like "Spaces" for focused conversations and video chat capabilities.
