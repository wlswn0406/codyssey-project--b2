- Microsoft has released a new AI 'code of conduct' to guide its models toward safe behavior, prohibiting actions like hacking systems or deceiving humans.

- This initiative highlights Microsoft's commitment to AI safety and control, addressing growing industry concerns about rogue AI and future superintelligent systems.
