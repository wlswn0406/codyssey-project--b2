### 요약 (Original)
- Elorian, a Palo Alto AI startup, has launched its first model, Glimpse, aiming to enable AI to reason *with* images rather than just *about* them.
- Glimpse introduces a "visual scratch space" mechanism, allowing it to re-engage with parts of an image (zoom, crop, measure) during reasoning for deeper understanding.
- This approach addresses current AI limitations in interpreting complex visual information like board games or instruction diagrams, developed efficiently and at a smaller scale.
