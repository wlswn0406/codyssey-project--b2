### 요약 (Original)
- Apple has launched a new study app in partnership with OpenEvidence to investigate how people utilize AI for health.
- The app offers clinically grounded answers to health questions, allows users to share HealthKit data, and aims to provide personalized insights.
- Targeting up to 200,000 U.S. participants, the study emphasizes robust data anonymization and privacy, explicitly stating data will not be used for AI model training.

- 이 앱은 건강 관련 질문에 임상 데이터 기반의 답변을 제공하며, HealthKit 데이터 공유를 통해 개인 맞춤형 정보를 제공합니다.
