### 요약 (Original)
- Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg stated the company's enterprise AI ambitions extend beyond just AI agents, to include APIs, direct compute sales, and other services.
- This strategy aims to create new revenue streams beyond advertising and subscriptions by initially serving its existing base of advertisers with AI tools.
- Meta also plans to offer its internal coding and productivity tools to a broader range of enterprise customers, acknowledging the shift requires "a different muscle."
