### 요약 (Original)
- Disney has agreed to hire the majority of Character.AI's workforce, offering jobs to at least 60 employees after the chatbot startup filed for bankruptcy.
- This follows Character.AI's former CEO becoming Disney's CTO and Disney's prior acquisition of some of the startup's assets for $20 million.
- Character.AI faced significant challenges including high operating costs, a lawsuit related to child safety, and government investigations, despite a large user base.
