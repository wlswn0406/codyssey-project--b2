### 요약 (Original)
- WindBorne Systems secured a substantial contract, potentially worth up to $49 million, with the U.S. Air Force for its autonomous weather balloon data.
- The company operates a fleet of 700 global weather balloons, collecting atmospheric data fed into its AI deep-learning weather forecasting model.
- This non-competitive contract, supporting growth and R&D, highlights WindBorne's unique capabilities and follows a recent $37 million Series B round and advanced forecasting model release.
