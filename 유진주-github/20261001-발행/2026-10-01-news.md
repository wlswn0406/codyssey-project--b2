### 요약 (Original)
- Samsung Electronics is significantly expanding the integration of agentic AI and physical AI across its business operations.
- This strategic move aims to transform AI intelligence into tangible performance and productivity enhancements.
- The company seeks to leverage autonomous AI systems for goal setting and execution, alongside AI-controlled physical robots, to drive efficiency and innovation.

- 삼성전자는 업무 전반에 걸쳐 에이전트(Agentic) AI와 피지컬(Physical) AI의 적용을 대폭 확대하고 있습니다.
